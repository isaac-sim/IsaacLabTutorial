reference/ <- source/isaaclab_tasks/isaaclab_tasks/contrib/microduck/

GA adaptations:
- integration.py authors BAM USD definitions, reactivates replaced prims, samples
  startup voltage, binds native actuators and connects the MJWarp solver channels.
  DOF/group selection and backlash binding derive from upstream actuator_control.py.
- bam.py gathers external load before computing torque, then publishes friction.
  The upstream motor, voltage, delay and friction equations are preserved.
- Imports use this project's namespace. Reference task IDs start with CourseReference.
- The reference uses GA's default_visualizer_cfg, OmegaConf resolver registration,
  and the GA actuator parameter API's controller component name.

These components came after GA and are maintained by this course. They are not
claimed to be included in Isaac Lab 3.0.0. The USD robot assets retain the upstream
asset URLs in robot.py. The learner builds tasks/ progressively through lessons.

config.py resolves the reference recipe's microduck_scale expressions after GA
preset/scalar overrides; GA otherwise skips interpolation on its fast path.
